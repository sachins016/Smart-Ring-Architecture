What Are Smart Rings?

Smart rings are compact wearable devices that fit on your finger and act as miniature smartwatches, capable of:

  - Tracking health metrics (heart rate, temperature, SpO₂, stress, motion)

  - Performing gesture recognition

  - Supporting contactless communication via Bluetooth or NFC. 
  
They integrate sensors, microcontrollers, power systems, and wireless modules into a sleek, ring-sized form factor.

Key Features

- Real-time health monitoring

- Gesture and motion recognition

- Wireless data transfer (BLE/NFC)

- Energy-efficient design

Applications

- Fitness and health tracking

- Sleep and stress monitoring

- Smart home control via gesture

- Contactless payments and authentication

- Biometric security
